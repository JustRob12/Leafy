## 🎨 Brand Tone & Visual Guidelines

* **Voice:** Friendly, relatable, modern, empowering, and clean. No intimidating finance jargon.
* **Core Philosophy:** Minimalist, private (offline-first), lightning-fast, and designed for real-world day-to-day spending.
* **Palette:**
  * Primary Green: `#10b981` (Emerald)
  * Dark Accent: `#064e3b` / Deep Forest
  * Backgrounds: Light Mode `#F8FAF9`, Dark Mode `#121212`
  * Text Colors: High Contrast Dark Charcoal `#1E293B`, Muted Grey `#64748B`

## 📐 Design & Layout Specifications

| Property | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Feed Aspect Ratio** | `1:1` (1080 × 1080 px) or `4:5` (1080 × 1350 px for Instagram carousel portrait) |
| **Story / Reel Cover Ratio**| `9:16` (1080 × 1920 px) |
| **Font Family** | Inter, Outfit, or Poppins (Heavy Bold for headlines, Medium for body) |
| **Visual Hierarchy** | 1. Category Badge (Top left or top pill)<br>2. Device Frame mockup with active feature UI (Center/Right)<br>3. Headline & 2-sentence description (High contrast text)<br>4. Brand watermark/logo (Bottom corner) |
| **Background Effect** | Subtle radial gradient `#10b981` at 15% opacity over dark or off-white background with subtle rounded card shadows |
